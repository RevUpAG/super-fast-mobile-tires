Five leaks → five fixes

Where calls are slipping away

1

Your website sends callers to another company

Found

The first thing on your homepage is a "Sponsored partner" banner for Golden Mobile Tires, with their phone number.

Every tap on that button is a $60–89 job you never see.

The fix

One number above the fold: yours. Call and $89 booking first, on every screen.

2

Google can't read your site

Found

It runs on a free subdomain, with no headings and no business data Google can understand.

Drivers searching "mobile tire near me" don't find you there.

The fix

Your own domain, proper SEO structure, a page for every city you serve, in English and Spanish.

3

Help is locked behind a signup

Found

The main button asks a stranded driver to create an account and start a $10/month membership before getting help.

At 2 AM on the shoulder, people don't sign up. They call the next result.

The fix

Help first: call, or $89 with no signup. Membership is offered after the job, when they're happy.

4

Google is confused about where you are

Found

Listings show Greenacres, Parkland, Deerfield Beach, Lake Worth and Delray Beach, and a similar "Super Fast Mobile Tire" in Atlanta shows up in searches.

Mixed signals make Google less likely to show you on the map.

The fix

One clean identity on Google, Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing and directories, plus a fully optimized Google profile.

5

Nothing is measured

Found

No analytics and no call tracking, so there's no way to know which search, page or city brings calls.

Money goes to guesses instead of the hours and areas that pay.

The fix

Every call and booking tracked, with a simple monthly report.
