FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need to sign up to get help?

No. Call (561) 255-8506 or book a one-time emergency call for $89 — no account, no subscription.

Do you charge more at night or on weekends?

No. Members pay $60 flat on core services and one-time calls are $89 — at 2PM or 2AM.

How fast can you get to me?

Average arrival is 30–45 minutes, and most jobs are finished in under 30 minutes once we're there.

What does the $10/month membership include?

$60 flat on tire change, flat repair, jump start, lockout, fuel delivery and wheel-lock removal, no after-hours surcharge, priority dispatch and live tracking. Cancel anytime.

Can you tow my car or fix something else?

Yes. Tow truck is a $25 dispatch fee plus quote; mobile mechanic is a $20 booking fee plus quote. Call for a quote on battery replacement, used tire install and rim repair.

Where do you work?

Palm Beach and Broward counties — from West Palm Beach down to Fort Lauderdale.

Stuck right now?

A real dispatcher answers 24/7. Tell us where you are — we come to you.
