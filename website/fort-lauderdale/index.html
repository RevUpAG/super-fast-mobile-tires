FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need to sign up to get help?

No. Call (561) 255-8506 or book a one-time emergency call for $89. No account, no subscription.

Do you charge more at night or on weekends?

No. One-time calls are $89 and members pay $60 flat on core services, at 2PM or 2AM.

How fast can you get to me?

Average arrival is 30–45 minutes, and most jobs are finished in under 30 minutes once we're there.

What does the $10/month membership include?

$60 flat on tire change, flat repair, jump start, lockout, fuel delivery and wheel-lock removal, no after-hours surcharge, priority dispatch and live GPS tracking. Cancel anytime.

Can you tow my car or fix something else?

Yes. Tow truck is a $25 dispatch fee plus a quote; mobile mechanic is a $20 booking fee plus a quote. Call for a quote on battery replacement, used tire install and rim repair.

Can I message you on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp (561) 255-8506 and send your location. Same number as our 24/7 dispatch line.

Palm Beach and Broward counties: West Palm Beach, Lake Worth, Greenacres, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Deerfield Beach, Parkland, Coral Springs, Margate, Pompano Beach and Fort Lauderdale.
